# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who need to send short AI prompts from constrained or unusual network environments. They value a fast, accountless tool and need clear feedback about which DNS transport ran, what failed, and what remains on their device.

## Product Purpose

DNSChat sends short prompts as DNS TXT queries and renders the returned text. Success means users can understand the transport, recover from failures, and trust the app's claims: no account, no API key, no tracking, encrypted local history, and no implication that observable DNS traffic is private.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and candid. It should feel like a refined native network instrument with the familiarity of a messenger.

## Anti-references

No neon hacker terminal, generic AI dashboard, fake privacy language, decorative glass everywhere, card-grid overload, floating-pill clutter, or playful bounce disconnected from state.

## Design Principles

- Make the signal path legible.
- State security boundaries where decisions happen.
- Keep conversation controls familiar and transport details progressive.
- Reserve emphasis and motion for meaningful state changes.
- Prefer native behavior and honest recovery over ornamental novelty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support screen readers, Dynamic Type, high contrast, light and dark schemes, keyboard navigation on web, minimum 44-point touch targets, bilingual English and Brazilian Portuguese copy, and reduced-motion paths that preserve state information.
